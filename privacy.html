Privacy policy: TrendingFacts Studio

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), TrendingFacts Studio uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload the channel's own videos and captions, change their visibility and descriptions, read the channel's own video statistics, and read comments on the channel's own videos.

Where data is kept

All data stays on the channel owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored there and is never sent to anyone else. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google user data

TrendingFacts Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
